How to set up.

Install the app, allow window control,
and save your first workspace.

  1. Install and open.

    Open GitHub Releases and download the app asset when available. Install Another Window Management in Applications. Open the app, then find its icon in your menu bar.

    macOS 13 or later

  2. Allow window control.

    Choose Allow Window Control… in the app. Follow its instructions to enable Another Window Management in macOS settings.

    If it’s missing, use + to add the app shown in Finder.

  3. Save your first workspace.

    Once setup is complete, choose Let’s start. Arrange your windows, then choose Save Current Workspace and give it a name.

What do I need to get started?

Another Window Management requires macOS 13 or later and permission to control windows. Open the app, choose Allow Window Control…, and follow the setup instructions.

Does a workspace restore every window?

A workspace reopens apps and positions their available windows. Depending on the app, you may be asked to open another window. Workspaces do not reopen specific documents or browser URLs.

Can I change the shortcuts and sizes?

Yes. Configure your keyboard shortcuts, size cycles, and gaps in points or percentages. The shortcuts in this website’s demo are illustrative; choose the combinations that work for you.

What happens when I disconnect a display?

When you restore a workspace without one of its saved displays, those windows are fitted onto the primary display. You can also associate workspaces with display arrangements and enable automatic restoration.

Can I back up my settings?

Yes. Workspaces and preferences are stored locally in a YAML file. Back up that file and restore it when needed.
